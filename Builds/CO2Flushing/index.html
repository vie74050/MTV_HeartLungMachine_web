This is a prototype of the CO2 flushing process. Refer to the CO2 Flushing document 🗗 provided by the instructor for more information.

v 2026.04.13 🗗

Overview

Phase 1: Set up

  • Perform hand hygiene
  • Attach bacterial air filter to the perfusion adapter on the CO2 tank
  • Ensure three-way stop cock open to circuit.
  • Using aseptic technique, connect the bacterial air filter to the arterial line pressure isolator three-way stop cock
  • Ensure three-way stop cock is open to circuit
  • Set the CO2 to 6 LPM

Phase 2: Oxygenator, Pump, and Reservoir Flush

    Direct CO2 retrograde through the Oxygenator
  • Clamp AV loop
    • Isolate flow to oxygenator -> pump -> reservoir only
  • Clamp blood cardioplegia 1/4" line
  • Clamp all 1/4' recirculation lines
  • Close manifold
  • Close oxygenator pre-membrane de-airing purge line
  • Allow 2 minutes of CO2 flushing.

Phase 3: AV Loop Flush

    Direct CO2 to AV Loop
  • Move clamp distal to where CO2 entry point on arterial line
  • Allow 30-45 seconds flushing
  • Return the clamp to proximal position on the arterial line

Phase 4: Cardioplegia Circuit Flush

    Direct CO2 to Cardioplegia via Oxygenator
  • Remove blood cardioplegia clamp and apply to oxygenator inlet
  • Allow 1 minute of CO2 flushing

Phase 5: Air Purge

  • Using aseptic technique, loosen caps on cardioplegia lines to purge air (~ 15 seconds)
  • Re-secure caps

Phase 6: Final Flush & Re-establish Circuit

  • Open oxygenator pre-membrane de-airing purge line
  • Open manifold
  • Remove all 1/4" recirculation line clamps
  • Remove oxygenator inlet clamp
  • Remove arterial line clamp

Phase 7: Completion

  • Turn off CO2, and fully close tank regulator
  • Remove and discard bacterial filter
  • Aseptically recap three-way stopcock with sterile cap
