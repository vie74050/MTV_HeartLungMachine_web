Overview

Set up

  • Perform hand hygiene
  • Attach bacterial air filter to the arterial line pressure isolator three-way stop cock.
  • Ensure three-way stop cock open to circuit.
  • Connect the CO2 supply hose to the system.
  • Set the CO2 to 6 LPM

Direct CO2 retrograde towards the Oxygenator

  • Clamp out the AV loop.

Direct CO2 retrograde through the Oxygenator, centrifugal pump and into reservoir only

  • Clamp out the cardioplegia system (#2)
  • Clamp out the recirculation line(s) #3
  • Close the manifold stopcock (#4)
  • Close the oxygenator pre-membrane de-airing purge line (#5)
  • Allow 2 minutes of CO2 flushing.

Direct CO2 to AV Loop

  • Move clamp (# 1) distal to where CO2 enters the CPB circuit on arterial line
  • Allow approx. 30-45 seconds of CO2 flushing

Direct CO2 to Cardioplegia Unit only, via Oxygenator

  • Return the clamp #1 to the proximal position on the arterial line
  • Remove blood cardioplegia ¼” line clamp (#2) and apply to oxygenator INLET (# 6)
  • Allow 1 minute of CO2 flushing
  • Purge air through cardioplegia crystalloid lines by loosening caps to allow air escape for approx. 15 seconds, reapply caps securely

Complete final CO2 Flush

  • Open oxygenator pre-membrane de-airing purge line (# 5)
  • Open manifold stopcock (#4)
  • Remove clamp from recirculation line(s) (#3)
  • Remove clamp from cardioplegia line (#6)
  • Remove clamp from arterial line (#1)
